Meet your therapist

Aloha, I’m Kali.

As a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist, I have more than 20 years of experience supporting individuals, couples and families across home, school, clinical and community settings.

I am here to wholeheartedly support you as you navigate unexpected challenges and grow into the person, couple or ʻohana you aspire to become. My role is to gently guide you as you explore your past, understand where you are today and work toward a healthier future.

Together, we will identify solutions that reflect your needs, experiences and goals. I applaud the courage it takes to seek support, and I look forward to walking alongside you and your ʻohana.

Kali IkemotoLicensed Marriage and Family Therapist

How Kali works

Personalized care, without rigid frameworks.

Kali practices integrative psychotherapy, meeting each client where they are and shaping therapy around their individual needs, goals and pace.

He blends the deeper insight of psychodynamic therapy with practical, present-moment tools from CBT, DBT and mindfulness. Together, Kali and each client create a thoughtful path toward healing and growth.

01

Integrative

Different therapeutic theories come together in one cohesive, personalized treatment plan.

Need immediate support?

Mālama Counseling Services is not an emergency or crisis-response service. If you or someone you know is experiencing an immediate emergency, call 911.
